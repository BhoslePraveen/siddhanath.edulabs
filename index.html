Why we started

Every great dream begins with a blank page.

A student preparing for NEET, JEE or CET fills thousands of pages over two years — formulas, mistakes, revision notes, shortcuts, diagrams and dreams.

Yet the notebooks on the market haven't changed in decades. They're made for everyone. Not for aspirants.
