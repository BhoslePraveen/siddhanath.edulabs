Why we started

Every great dream begins with a blank page.

A student preparing for NEET, JEE or CET fills thousands of pages over two years — formulas, mistakes, revision notes, shortcuts, diagrams and dreams.

Yet the notebooks on the market haven't changed in decades. They're made for everyone. Not for aspirants.

“What if a notebook itself could become a study companion?”

That question became Laksh.

Built for the aspirant journey

Start with a blank page. Write the way you study. Track, revise, recall. No generic ruled sheets — every spread designed for aspirants.

Note-taking → Organization → Revision → Recall
Our mission

We aren't building stationery.
We're reimagining how India's aspirants learn, organize and revise.

Every page is designed after studying how students actually work — from organized note-taking to revision planning, from formula references to smart layouts. Every detail has one purpose: to help you study better.

What makes Laksh different

Nine details, designed around how aspirants actually study.

Not a generic notebook with a NEET/JEE label on it — every page inside earns its place.

Mistake logs

A dedicated place to log recurring mistakes by subject, so you stop repeating them in the exam.

Mock test tracker

Log every mock score in one place and watch your trend line before the real exam.

Chapter-wise tracker

See completion and revision status across every NEET/JEE/CET chapter at a glance.

Ruled + grid combo

One notebook, two page styles — ruled pages for notes, grid pages for graphs, diagrams and structures.

Habit tracker

Build and track the daily study habits that actually decide your rank — not just your syllabus.

Daily work tracker

Plan and log what you study each day, so nothing falls through the cracks before your exam.

300+ pages

Enough room for a full subject's notes, without switching notebooks halfway through the syllabus.

Subject-wise books

Separate volumes for Physics, Chemistry and Maths/Biology, so your notes never get mixed up.

Essential stationery included

Comes with the basics you need to start writing on day one — no last-minute stationery run.
